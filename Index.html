About Our Mission

Cancer Awareness & Support is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing resources, emotional support, and educational materials to individuals and families affected by cancer.

We believe in the power of community, hope, and knowledge to overcome the challenges of a cancer diagnosis. Our team includes survivors, healthcare professionals, and volunteers committed to making a difference.
